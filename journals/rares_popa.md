# Personal Journal - Rares Popa

## Week 1 (7 - 13 September 2026)

### What I did this week

- **Wednesday 9 September - TA meeting (25 min).** The whole team met the TA. We
  explained the idea we had at that moment and asked what is expected from us for the
  project and for the proof of concept.
- **Saturday 12 September - team meeting (2h).** We started by going through the
  deliverables of the project and agreed on a timeline for them, so that everyone knows
  what has to be ready and by when. After that we moved to the idea itself, and while
  discussing it in detail we noticed that each of us was describing the application in a
  different way. Instead of settling it quickly in the meeting, we decided it deserved
  proper work: the task until Sunday was that everyone writes down their own detailed
  version of how the app should work.
- **Saturday evening and Sunday morning - work on my own.** I wrote down concretely how
  I think the application should work: who uses it, what happens step by step, and what
  the app has to do in each case. I also went through the use cases we could support and
  checked which technologies exist for the parts I was unsure about, so that on Sunday I
  could say what is realistic to build and not only what sounds good.
- **Sunday 13 September - team meeting (2h).** Everyone presented what they had prepared.
  Because we all came with something written, the discussion was much easier than on
  Saturday and we agreed on one version of the idea and on the scope. At the end we split
  the work into clear tasks, each with an owner, to be finished by the next meeting.
